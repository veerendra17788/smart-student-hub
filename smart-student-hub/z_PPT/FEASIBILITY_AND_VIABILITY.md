```
Blockchain Scalability Issues:
  - Risk Level: Medium
  - Impact: High
  - Mitigation: Layer 2 solutions, hybrid approach
  - Contingency: Traditional database backup

AI Model Accuracy Concerns:
  - Risk Level: Medium
  - Impact: Medium
  - Mitigation: Human oversight, multiple AI providers
  - Contingency: Manual review processes

Data Security Breaches:
  - Risk Level: Low
  - Impact: Very High
  - Mitigation: End-to-end encryption, regular audits
  - Contingency: Incident response plan, insurance
```
